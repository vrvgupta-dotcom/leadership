About

An entrepreneur and IIM Ahmedabad alumnus who, after experiencing institutional failure firsthand, converted that experience into a live governance and leadership case now used with MBA and executive audiences globally. His dual track record — a self-represented constitutional petition before the Delhi High Court, and a parallel individual communication to the UN OHCHR on India's non-compliance with the UNCRPD — gives the session an analytical rigour most leadership cases don't have.

Designed for courses in

  • Leadership & organisational behaviour
  • Business ethics & corporate governance
  • ESG, inclusion & stakeholder accountability
  • Emerging-market strategy & risk
  • Executive leadership development

Speaking Topics

01

Leadership

Leadership Courage Under Constraint

Decision-making when power asymmetry, personal risk, and institutional opacity are all high — what the case reveals about the conditions under which leaders act, and those under which they don't.

02

Governance

Inclusion as a Governance Issue

How exclusion signals deeper governance failure — and creates legal, reputational, and operational risk. Inclusion reframed not as an equity imperative but as a test of institutional design quality.

03

Emerging Markets

Risk Management in Emerging Markets

Navigating weak enforcement, bureaucratic discretion, and informal power structures in developing-country contexts — from inside the experience rather than as an external observer.

04

Strategy & Advocacy

Resilience & Advocacy as Leadership Tools

How the UN petition and the Delhi HC case were each built as deliberate, structural responses to institutional failure — not personal grievance, but strategic accountability mechanisms.

These sessions are candid, analytical, and interactive — not motivational talks. Designed for MBA and executive audiences who want a working case study, not an inspiration story.

Governance Record

Delhi High Court · Constitutional Petition

W.P.(C) 11495/2025

Self-represented constitutional petition on custodial disability rights violations by Delhi Police and Tihar Jail. Built as a deliberate accountability mechanism — using institutional process to force institutional reckoning.

UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights · Individual Communication

An individual communication to the UN OHCHR on India's non-compliance with the UNCRPD — one of only three such communications from a disabled Indian. A parallel track to the Delhi HC petition, chosen deliberately to build accountability at the international level simultaneously.
